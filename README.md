## Selected work

### DriftLess

[DriftLess](https://driftless.chat) is a guided website builder for independent makers. A maker answers questions, receives an authored private website, edits it without code, and decides when to publish.

I lead the product direction, specifications, acceptance criteria, and release decisions. The project is also my practical laboratory for learning where AI agents help, where they fail, and which controls are needed to ship reliable customer-facing work.

### Enterprise AI delivery

At CEVA Logistics and Bolloré Transport & Logistics, I led AI initiatives across international operations from problem framing through rollout and adoption.

- **Chat with Data:** reduced the wait for operational answers from days to under 20 seconds, then supported sustained use through on-site power-user training.
- **Forecast & Adapt:** coordinated delivery with Capgemini so volume forecasts became staffing recommendations under local labour constraints; delivered on time and on budget.
- Kept concurrent initiatives moving through siloed systems, a delayed central data platform, conflicting incentives, and a mid-project AI-provider change.
